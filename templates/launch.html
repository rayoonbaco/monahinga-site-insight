Launch Thesis

Information used to be scarce. Now the advantage is turning abundant information into usable intelligence.

Monahinga is a working proof of that idea: selected land becomes an intelligence surface, terrain becomes visual evidence, and AI role specialists help synthesize complex signals into action-ready review material.

What this preview claims

  • It can create a terrain-review workflow from a selected place.
  • It can visualize multiple terrain derivatives and 3D context.
  • It can organize candidate observations into cautious decision-support language.
  • It can prepare better review questions for archaeologists, planners, and domain experts.

What it does not claim yet

  • It does not confirm archaeological sites from terrain alone.
  • It does not replace fieldwork, licensed experts, or local review.
  • It does not pretend every layer is equally reliable in every geography.
  • It is a proof-of-direction preview, not a finished commercial product.

Best public demo path

  1. Open the launch preview and explain the thesis.
  2. Draw or open a real area on the map.
  3. Show the 3D terrain and derivative layer grid.
  4. Open the archaeology synthesis panel and emphasize candidate-feature language.
  5. Close with the larger operating model: terrain intelligence can support archaeology, land development, insurance, and other decision domains.
